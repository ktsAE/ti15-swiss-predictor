Swiss Elimination

Group stage

Modified Swiss · Bo3 13–16 Aug

Win four and you are through; lose four and you are out, and you stop playing the moment either lands. The 16 teams are split into two groups of eight: rounds 1–3 are played inside your own group, round 4 only against the other group, round 5 is open. Within that, teams always meet someone on their exact record, and the pairing minimises the gap in the standings. Rounds Valve has already drawn are shown as drawn; the rest follow the rules from wherever your picks leave off.

Elimination round

Teams pick opponents · Bo3 16 Aug

No seeded ladder here — the teams choose. The best 3–2 team picks whichever 2–3 team it wants to face, then the next best picks from those left, and so on down. Five winners join the three teams already through.

Where this comes from

The draw is Valve's own. Every round Valve has published is taken from its bracket exactly as drawn — marked Official draw above the round — so nothing you can see here is a guess at a matchup that has already been announced. Rounds Valve has not drawn yet are worked out from the published rules, in order: same record, avoid repeat pairings, minimise the gap in the standings — with the per-round group restrictions, and round 5 reversed to maximise the gap in matches where the loser is knocked out. That part is a projection, and the moment you start picking winners yourself every round after your first pick is derived, because the bracket is then yours rather than Valve's. Rules and results come from Valve's league API, cross-checked against the Liquipedia TI 2026 pages. Teams appear under their TI names (BoomBoys, Iron Wing, TEAM VISION, HULIGANI).

How teams are ranked — Valve's order is matches won, matches lost, percentage of games won, opponents' total wins, then opponents' average game win percentage. All five are here, which matters more than it sounds: everyone in a pairing bucket has the same record by definition, so the maps they dropped getting there are usually the first thing that separates them. The chain ends in average game duration and a coin toss, which this tool cannot know, so anything still level falls back to a fixed order. A series you picked yourself has no map score, and counts as 2–1.

Where it stops — the tool goes as far as Valve's published information does: it names the eight teams who reach the main event, but not their bracket seeding, which has not been announced.

Results already played are marked final and cannot be clicked. Those come from the RESULTS table at the top of the script, filled in as the tournament goes; everything after them is still yours to pick.
